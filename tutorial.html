Welcome aboard

Every film you watch, in one high-altitude view.

Cirrus keeps your watchlist, your watch history, and your own ratings in one calm place — no accounts, no backend, nothing to sync. Add movies by searching or typing them in by hand, log what you thought once you've watched them, and compare your taste against the critics whenever you're curious. Here's the whole thing in about three minutes.

Add a movie Watchlist & Watched Your rating Sourced ratings Reviews Discover new releases Notes & history Backup & settings
  1. 1

    Add a movie

    Type a title into the search bar and pick it from the results — Cirrus pulls in the poster, year, and overview automatically. No TMDB key yet, or can't find something obscure? Hit Add Manually instead and just type the title and year.

    Search and auto-filled details need a free TMDB API key, added once in Settings. Everything else works fine without one.

  2. 2

    Watchlist and Watched

    Every movie starts on your Watchlist. Open it and flip the status to Watched once you've seen it, with the date you watched it — so years from now you're never left wondering whether you've already seen something.

    🎬 Watchlist ✅ Watched 🔭 Discover
  3. 3

    Rate it yourself

    Open any movie's details and tap the stars to set your own 1–5 rating. It shows right on the card, so your whole collection is scannable at a glance.

  4. 4

    Compare against the critics

    Each movie's details show a TMDB score automatically. Add a free OMDb API key in Settings too, and Cirrus pulls in IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic ratings alongside it — so you can see exactly how your take lines up with your favorite sources.

  5. 5

    Read a few reviews

    A handful of written reviews are pulled into each movie's detail view when a TMDB key is set, so you can get a feel for the reaction without leaving the app.

  6. 6

    Discover new releases

    The Discover tab shows what's currently in theaters, straight from TMDB. Tap anything there to drop it onto your watchlist.

  7. 7

    Keep your own notes

    Every movie has a free-text notes field in its details — jot down who you watched it with, a favorite line, or why you gave it the rating you did.

  8. 8

    Back up your collection

    Cirrus stores everything only in this browser's local storage. Use Export JSON in Settings regularly to save a backup, and Import JSON to restore it anywhere. Settings also has day, twilight, and night atmospheres to match your sky.

That's the whole app.
